About MarinOS

MarinOS is the directory for apps and documentation built by County of Marin teams. Each product links back here from its own MarinOS banner menu.

Building on MarinOS

  • marin-app-template — start a new app here
  • marin-ui — shared components, tokens, and app shell
  • marin-digital-standards — accessibility, content, brand, and product-design requirements
  • marin-skills — AI workflows for building and maintaining Marin applications
